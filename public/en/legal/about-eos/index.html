Legal notice

Who publishes this site, under what licence, and what E-OS is in relation to Redox OS.

Status: in force · Last reviewed: 2026-09-03 · Owner: Gh0s777tt

Publisher

E-OS is an independent open-source project maintained by one person. There is no company, no legal entity, no registered address and no funding. The copyright line reads "Damian (Gh0s777tt) and the E-OS contributors".

Provenance

E-OS is a downstream distribution derived from Redox OS, the Rust microkernel operating system created by Jeremy Soller and the Redox OS community. The kernel, relibc, RedoxFS, the driver stack and the cookbook build system originate there and remain under the MIT licence.

Relationship to Redox OS

None, formally. E-OS is not endorsed by, affiliated with or a product of the Redox OS project. It tracks upstream through pinned revisions and carries its patches in forks that are kept rebasable, so that the relationship stays a technical one.

This site

Static pages generated from the project's own documents and build configuration, hosted on GitLab Pages. Its source is a separate repository under the same licence as the project.

Contact

The maintainer address in the security policy is the contact for everything: security reports, legal questions and press. There is no other channel and no press office.
