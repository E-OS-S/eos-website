Trademarks

Redox OS is somebody else's trademark. E-OS is a downstream distribution, not official Redox OS software, and this page states the difference precisely.

Status: in force · Last reviewed: 2026-09-03 · Owner: Gh0s777tt

The Redox OS trademark

The Redox OS trademark — the name, the logo and the associated marks — belongs to the Redox OS nonprofit and is governed by their trademark policy, which took effect on 3 December 2025. E-OS follows that policy, and where this page and that policy differ, that policy governs.

How E-OS refers to Redox OS

Descriptively, to say what E-OS is built on. E-OS is third-party software for the Redox OS operating system. It does not use the Redox OS name or logo in a way that suggests official status, endorsement or sponsorship, and it does not alter the logo or fold it into a mark of its own.

Namespaces E-OS does not use

Official Redox OS software may use the redox-os- package namespace and the org.redox_os. reverse-DNS prefix. E-OS is not official Redox OS software and uses neither.

The E-OS name

E-OS, and the Crimson naming of the desktop, are the project's own. The NOTICE file reserves them; there is no registered mark and no legal entity to hold one. Use the name to refer to this project, and do not use it in a way that suggests a fork or a rebuild is the same software.

Third-party names

The COSMIC applications and icon themes are System76's. NetSurf belongs to the NetSurf developers. Rust and Cargo are the Rust Foundation's. Naming them here is descriptive use and implies no endorsement in either direction.

What you may and may not do

Summarised from the upstream policy and from how E-OS reads it. The full text is linked below and takes precedence over this summary.

  • You may say that your project runs on, extends or derives from E-OS, and that E-OS is a Redox OS downstream.
  • You may reproduce this documentation under the project's licence, including the names in it.
  • You may not imply that the Redox OS nonprofit endorses E-OS, or that E-OS is official Redox OS software. It is neither.
  • You may not use the Redox OS logo modified, or as part of another mark, without written permission from the Redox OS nonprofit.
  • You may not use the Redox OS trademark on merchandise for commercial purposes without their authorisation.
  • If you fork E-OS and change it, do not present the result as E-OS. Rename it, as you would with any distribution.

Who to ask

Questions about the Redox OS trademark go to the Redox OS nonprofit, not to this project. Questions about the E-OS name go to the maintainer.
