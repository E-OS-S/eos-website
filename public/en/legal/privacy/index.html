Privacy policy

This site collects nothing. The detail below exists so that you do not have to take that on trust.

Status: in force · Last reviewed: 2026-09-03 · Owner: Gh0s777tt

What this site stores

One entry in your browser's localStorage, named eos-theme, holding the string light or dark. It never leaves your device, is never read by the project, and the site works correctly when it is absent or when storage is blocked entirely.

What this site does not do

No cookies. No analytics. No trackers, tag managers or advertising. No fonts, scripts, stylesheets or images loaded from a third-party host: everything a page needs is served from the same origin as the page.

Server logs

The pages are served by GitLab Pages. Like any web server, that host processes your request, which includes your address and user agent, under its own operator's policy. The project has no access to those logs and does not receive them.

Links away from this site

Links to GitLab, GitHub and Redox OS leave this site. From the moment you follow one, that operator's privacy policy applies and this one does not.

Tickets

The support page composes a link; it transmits nothing. When you follow that link you are on GitLab, and GitLab's terms and privacy policy apply. Issues created there are public, including anything pasted into them.

Contact and changes

Questions about this policy go to the maintainer address in the security policy. This page carries a review date; a material change moves that date and appears in the site repository's history.
